# Waga — Pitch script (template-aligned)

Follows the YOMYO / CFI pitch outline, tightened for a hackathon room (~4–5 min).  
Open `pitch/index.html`. Advance with → or space.

**Before you present:** fill real names on the Founders slide.

---

### 1. Title
“We’re **Waga** — AI-assisted food affordability monitoring for humanitarian and commercial decisions in Ethiopia.”

### 2. Hook
“Can we keep deciding on last month’s prices? Cash moves weekly. The data still arrives monthly. Households feel that gap first — including us.”

### 3. Agenda
“We’ll walk founders → overview → problem → market → value → vision → sizing → competition → model → ask.”

### 4. Founders
“**Insight:** oil and staples moved in our kitchens before any institution published a number.  
**Advantage:** we are from here — Amharic, Afaan Oromo, and we can hold a Merkato agent network.  
**Proof:** backend, Telegram intake with local speech-to-text, reputation and human review — already shipped.  
*[Name your team and the empty seat.]*”

### 5. Business overview
“We do **verified food-price intelligence** for **cash programmes and food buyers** by uniquely **holding a trusted local agent network at a 72-hour cadence**.  
Four pillars: speed, never impute, full audit chain, cited AI.”

### 6. Problem
“Ethiopia’s food prices move weekly. The data that describes them moves monthly.  
Officers pad monthly factsheets with WhatsApp guesses. The market has already moved.”

### 7. Market opportunity
“**Pain:** mis-set transfers starve families or waste donor money.  
**Need:** voice intake in local languages + an auditable index — now possible.  
**Why now:** food inflation 15.1%, collapsed humanitarian budgets, more cash aid — fewer staff, higher stakes.”

### 8. Value proposition
“We don’t sell prices. We sell a **decision that is defensible in writing**.  
Agent → human review → 72-hour index → copilot that answers *how should we adjust cash?* with citations.”

### 9. Proof
“Basket +18.9% in 30 days. Off by 781 birr × 50,000 households = **39 million birr a month**.  
Same-day Addis markets differ 14–26%. We never impute. Payback is obvious: one avoided bad transfer decision.”

### 10. Vision & mission
“**Mission:** make the real cost of food visible fast enough to act on.  
**Vision:** the reference price layer for East African markets — fastest number anyone can audit.  
Addis depth first, then cities, then the next JMMI country.”

### 11. Market sizing
“TAM ~$59M global monitoring layer. Ethiopia SAM ~$5.2M. Addis SOM ~$0.3M by year 3.  
Focused, capital-efficient — not a fake billion-dollar slide.”

### 12. Competition
“JMMI and WFP are free and monthly — we complement them.  
The real incumbent is Excel plus guesswork.  
**Barrier:** the scored agent network. Software copies; Merkato trust doesn’t.”

### 13. Business model
“Create verified prices. Deliver NGO / business / research products.  
Capture via SaaS. Defend with the network and audit trail. Public lookup free — never ads.”

### 14. Go to market
“NGOs first via the Cash Working Group — free pilots, convert on quarterly transfer reviews.  
Then businesses on a quote-benchmark hook. Agents ahead of sales.”

### 15. Financials
“Break even on **four NGO customers**. Year-1 lean cost ~$2k/mo. Profitable small, ~$300k ARR by year 3.”

### 16. Ask
“**Hackathon:** intros to the Cash Working Group and feedback on the live loop.  
**Capital path:** $150k / 18 months — agents, product, three NGO pilots → break-even by month 14.  
**Waga** — the price layer for the week you’re deciding, not the month that already passed.”
