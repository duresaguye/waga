Waga

AI-assisted food affordability monitoring for Ethiopia — fast enough to act on.

Ethiopian Cursor Hackathon · Addis Ababa

The problem

Ethiopia’s food prices move weekly. The data that describes them moves monthly.

An NGO officer setting cash transfers for 50,000 households still works from last month’s basket — while markets have already moved.

Cost of being wrong

39M birr One month. One transfer value. Off by 781 birr per household across 50,000 families — from an 18.9% basket jump in 30 days.

Either families cannot buy the basket, or scarce donor money is overspent. Both are failures — and neither shows up until monitoring arrives months later.

The product

Verified market data that becomes a defensible decision.

Price network

Telegram agents submit in Amharic, Afaan Oromo, or English — text or voice.

Affordability score

72-hour staple basket cost and change for Addis markets.

Market heat map

Named markets, not citywide averages that hide 14–26% spreads.

AI copilot

Answers: “How should we adjust cash assistance?” — with citations.

How it works

From Merkato voice note to transfer recommendation.

Phase 1: Addis · 9 markets · 5 staples. Publish only when a cell has ≥3 accepted submissions.

Where AI earns its place

AI never invents a price.

Language intake

Amharic & Afaan Oromo speech-to-text live in Telegram — agent confirms before save.

Spike detection

Detrended ALPS-style alerts daily — not monthly. We fixed false “crises” on rising markets.

Situation briefs

Weekly NGO brief: drivers, hot markets, adjustment band, birr impact of doing nothing.

Cited copilot

Rules produce numbers; the model only narrates. No citation = treated as a bug.

Restraint is the product: no forecasting, no imputation, no silent guesses.

Proof, not slides
