Problem statement

Ethiopia’s food prices move weekly. The data that describes them moves monthly.

An NGO officer setting transfers for 50,000 households works from a monthly JMMI factsheet, then pads the number from WhatsApp. By then the market has already moved.

Market opportunity

Business pain. Technology need. Urgency now.

Business pain

Stale baskets mis-set cash values — underfeeding families or burning scarce donor money.

Technology need

Amharic/Oromo voice intake + auditable index finally possible via Telegram and local STT APIs.

Why now

Food inflation 15.1% (Jun 2026). Humanitarian budgets collapsed. Fewer analysts, higher stakes per birr.

Tailwind

Cash is displacing in-kind aid — every birr shifted to CVA raises the value of accurate prices.

Vision & mission

Mission
Make the real cost of food in Ethiopian markets visible fast enough to act on.
Vision
Become the reference price layer for East African markets — the number everyone cites because it is the fastest one anybody can audit.

Market evolution: more CVA, thinner coordination, demand for independent, auditable signal. We lead with Addis depth first, then cities, then the next JMMI country.
